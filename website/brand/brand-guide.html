Brand book · v1 · November 2026
Together in every prayer.

Lovinity is a private daily companion for a Muslim husband and wife: prayer side by side, a shared week, small daily moments, and a chat only the two of them can read. This book sets out how Lovinity looks, sounds and moves, wherever it appears.

وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً

“And He placed between you affection and mercy.” Qur’an 30:21. The idea the whole brand grows from.

The idea

Two lives, one shared space

The gold ring is you. In the app, everything that is yours is gold: your prayers, your plans, your messages.

The rose ring is your spouse. Their prayers, plans and moments are always rose, so a glance tells you whose is whose.

The overlap glows, because that is what the app is for: the space only the two of you share. A crescent sits at its centre, because faith sits at the centre of the marriage.

The name. Lovinity is love and infinity: a marriage meant to last beyond this life.

Logo

One mark, three lockups

Use the horizontal logo by default. Use the mark alone where the name is already present, such as the app icon, a favicon or a social avatar. The wordmark is Space Grotesk Medium with spacing tightened by 2%, drawn as outlines, so it never depends on an installed font.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo at least the height of the crescent on every side. The dashed line shows the minimum.

Minimum size

Horizontal logo: 96px wide on screen, 25mm in print. Mark alone: 16px. Below that, the two rings blur into one.

Backgrounds

Use the dark version on Midnight and every dark theme, and the light version on Sandstone, Pearl and white. On photos, place the logo on a calm, dark area, never over busy detail.

Don’t

✕ Swap the ring colours. Gold is always you, rose is always the spouse.
✕ Recolour, stretch, outline or add drop shadows.
✕ Replace the crescent with a heart or any other symbol.
✕ Put the dark logo on a light background, or the reverse.
App icon

The mark, lit from within

The mark on Midnight, with a soft gold glow behind the overlap. iOS and Android round the corners themselves, so the master file is a full square.

It replaces the current illustrated icon. That artwork, with the gold L, the infinity loop and the praying couple, can still appear as a hero illustration, but it can’t work as an icon. Its fine figures disappear at small sizes, and a mark built on people won’t suit everyone in the audience.

Colour

Midnight and gold, with rose for the spouse

Midnight Gold is the brand’s home: near-black, warm paper-white text, and gold reserved for what matters most. Rose belongs only to the spouse. Never use it as a general accent.

Typography

Three typefaces

Space Grotesk

Display. Headlines, numbers, the wordmark. Medium weight, letter spacing tightened by 2–3%.

Inter

Text. Everything people read: body, buttons, labels. Regular to Semibold.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Amiri. Every Arabic verse, dua and prayer name, always with the English beside it.

Voice

Warm, calm and exact

Lovinity speaks like a kind friend who knows the deen well: gentle, never preachy, and never cute about something sacred. It states facts plainly, especially about privacy, and never promises what the app doesn’t do.

Say

“Naziha prayed Asr. Maghrib is next, at 5:04.”

Not

“Your bae just crushed Asr! 🔥🔥”

Say

“Only the two of you can read this chat.”

Not

“Military-grade, unhackable security.”

Say

“Her prayers are excused today. A little extra kindness goes a long way.”

Not

“She’s on her period 🩸”

Words we use

  • your spouse, husband, wife
  • your space of two
  • pray side by side, say Ameen together
  • moments, letters, your wall

Words we avoid

  • bae, soulmate, “partner” in marketing copy
  • dating, match, swipe
  • guilt about missed prayers
  • “AI-powered”, “revolutionary”

Emoji

At most one per message, and only where the app already uses them: 🤲 for duas, 📸 for moments, 🤍 for care. Never in headlines.

Imagery and motion

Show the product, lit with gold

Real screens only

Marketing shows the real app, captured from a device in the matching theme. No mock features and no invented reviews.

No faces

No stock couples and no drawn figures. Show light, hands, objects and moments: a prayer mat at Fajr, two cups of chai, a crescent through a window.

Gold light, not gold paint

Gold appears as light: a glow, a fine line, the overlap of the rings. Avoid gold textures, glitter and bevels.

Motion that breathes

Soft springs of about 300–600ms. The rings draw themselves, themes spread out from where you tap, numbers roll. One moving idea per screen, and nothing moves for people who turned motion off.
